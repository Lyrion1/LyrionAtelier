What is a good gift for a Gemini?

Go for pieces with a line they can quote. Gemini loves a conversation-starter, and a reading gives them something to talk about for weeks. Our Gemini picks below start at 34 GBP and show in your own currency.

When is Gemini season?

Gemini season runs May 21 – June 20. Order posted pieces at least 10 to 14 days before a birthday; readings and digital certificates are emailed, so they work as last-minute gifts.

What if I don't know their exact birth time?

Sun-sign pieces only need the birthday. For readings, a birth time makes the chart more precise, but we can write from the date and place alone and will say so in the reading.
